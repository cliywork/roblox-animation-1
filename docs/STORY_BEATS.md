## Owner's special instructions hidden in the beats

- **21.45–24.51:** three movie shots are merged into **one** shot (top angle over the blasts, one
  push-in). Built as shot 130.
- **37.91–41.46:** Iron Man is pushed back by the beam with **no body animation**, carried by heavy VFX.
- **44.56–45.0:** just his fist with the new weapon forming, no body animation.
- **"the missing second" (52.3–52.9):** the **face-off**, symmetric two-shot, used as the
  **YouTube thumbnail**. Built as shot 360 (52.267–52.768).
- **01:13.7 beat-knock:** the owner's 0.06 s is a typo; the knock-away happens **twice** ("repeat
  above animation 2 times"). Find the real cut times with cut detection.
- **01:16.5–01:17.9:** "two character animation, be careful": a close struggle; contacts must touch.
- **01:17.9–01:19.2:** the nanotech **sword grows out of his armour**. The agent builds it (parts /
  mesh), the owner cannot model it.
- **01:21–01:22.6:** the stab; **the video ends on this cut**.
- Dialogue is shown as **subtitles only** (no movie audio — copyright). Lines so far are in
  `SUBS` in scene.py. Still to add: Thanos' line at 01:19.2 ("that's coming…" — check the clip).
